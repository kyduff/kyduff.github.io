These are my favorite books on business. In my honest opinion, if you're interested in business, it's worth dropping pretty much anything else to read these books immediately.

* **My Life and Work**
* **38 Letters from J.D. Rockefeller to his son**
* **Poor Charlie’s Almanack**
* **My Life in Advertising**
